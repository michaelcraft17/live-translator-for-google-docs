Connecting to the document…

Open a Google Doc, then use the on-page 🌐 button (top right) or the button above to open the translation side panel. Paste your own Google Cloud Translation or DeepL API key above — the extension sends document text to whichever service you pick, and to nobody else. The third option talks to an undocumented Google endpoint that needs no key but carries no terms of service or reliability guarantee; it is there for local development, not everyday use. Turn off scroll sync to scroll the original and the translation independently — useful if a highlight ever lands on the wrong sentence and auto-scrolling would otherwise fight you while you look for the right one.
