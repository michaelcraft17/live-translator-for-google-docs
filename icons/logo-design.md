## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Chrome extension icon for Live Translator for Google Docs. Create one polished standalone square app logo, a bold minimal pair of overlapping document/panel tiles conveying live translation and side-by-side reading. Front tile with a large beautifully rendered white A, second tile with a simple translation language glyph 文. Strong cobalt blue and teal, clean flat geometric forms, softly rounded corners. Extremely clear silhouette and thick shapes for readability at 16 to 128 pixels. Fill about 88 percent of the square canvas, balanced centered composition. Transparent outside the mark. No shadows, no fine detail, no gradients, no mockup, no surrounding typography, no Google logo, no watermark. Deliver a single icon only.
